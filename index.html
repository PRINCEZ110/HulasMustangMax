THE MUSTANG MAX SERIES.
NEPAL'S JEEP OF 1998–2013.

Introduction

Developed from necessity in Biratnagar, the Hulas Mustang of 1998 was Nepal's first diesel-engine jeep. Built for hills and plains alike, it became an icon of made-in-Nepal engineering.

Production Models

Engineers under chief engineer Prafulla Das assembled the future Mustang from parts sourced from India and China, aiming for at least 150 jeeps a year.

Testimonials / Story

What people said at the time.

“

The Mustang isn't about comfort, it's about reaching. Hard. Loaded. Every day. A hill-legal workhorse that feels more at home on a dozer track than a boulevard.

Prafulla Das, former GM & chief engineer, Hulas Motors: “Let's not reopen old wounds. Whenever I think about it, I feel a bit sad… As a noble citizen, I wanted to do something for the country.”

Das spent two decades on the dream. PM Baburam Bhattarai's team asked for a VVIP car — comfortable, secure, fast. In two months his team built the air-conditioned Mustang Max. Bhattarai used Ba 1 Jha 9360 for 20 months.

Next was Hulas Bravo for Rs 2–3M to compete with Rs 6–10M imports. Over Rs 8M went into research. Then the 2012 budget raised registration to Euro-3 only. The team asked for two years to comply, got one. Production stopped. Bravo never reached the road.
