THE MUSTANG MAX SERIES.
NEPAL'S JEEP OF 1998–2013.

Introduction

Developed from necessity, the Hulas Mustang of 1998 was the first of its kind in Nepal. And with its dozer-track reputation in hills and plains, it quickly became the most iconic made-in-Nepal workhorse.

Production Models

Engineers under chief engineer Prafulla Das supplied the future Mustang with the best usable parts for Nepali roads.

Testimonials / Story

What people said at the time.

The Mustang isn't about transportation, it's about reaching. Hard. Loaded. Every day. A hill-legal workhorse more at home on a dozer track than a boulevard.

Prafulla Das (summary): “Let's not reopen old wounds… As a noble citizen, I wanted to do something for the country.” Two decades of work, two months to build the AC VVIP Mustang Max.

Hulas Bravo (Rs 2–3M, 11 seats) took Rs 8M+ R&D to fight Rs 6–10M imports. The 2012 budget allowed only Euro-3 registrations. Team asked for 2 years, got 1. Production stopped 2013. Bravo never reached the road.
